# Project Presentation: TransperenC
**Redefining NGO Accountability through Real-Time Transparency**

---

## 1. Project Overview
**TransperenC** is a mission-driven Android application built to eliminate the "Trust Gap" between Non-Profit Organizations (NGOs) and their donors. Unlike traditional reporting which is annual and static, TransperenC offers a **Live Audited Ledger** that provides real-time visibility into every cent donated and spent.

## 2. The Problem: The Transparency Gap
In the current philanthropic landscape:
*   **Donors** feel disconnected from the impact of their money.
*   **NGOs** struggle with fragmented data and slow reporting cycles.
*   **Trust** is eroded by lack of verifiable spending data.

## 3. The Solution: A Live Accountability Ecosystem
TransperenC acts as a "Trust Layer" by:
*   Linking every donation directly to specific project budgets.
*   Providing a public-facing ledger that updates instantly.
*   Using gamification to reward NGOs for frequent, accurate reporting.

## 4. Key Features Explained

### A. Live Audited Ledger (NGO Dashboard)
*   **What it is:** A real-time transaction history for the organization.
*   **How it helps:** It mimics blockchain transparency. Every entry (TXN) is assigned a reference ID and status (e.g., "CLEARED"), making the NGO's financial health visible to stakeholders at a glance.

### B. Donor Portal & Secure Contributions
*   **What it is:** A dedicated gateway for supporters to browse active projects.
*   **How it helps:** Donors can choose exactly where their money goes. The portal handles simulated payments and automatically generates Section 18A tax certificates, professionalizing the donation experience.

### C. NGO Compliance Score
*   **What it is:** A dynamic percentage-based metric (0-100%).
*   **How it helps:** The app automatically calculates a score based on how frequently the NGO uploads documents, how healthy their project budgets are, and how much data they provide. This creates a "Trust Rating" that donors can verify.

### D. "Transparency Warriors" (Gamification)
*   **What it is:** An XP (Experience Points) and Leveling system.
*   **How it helps:** NGOs earn XP for every transparency action (e.g., filing a report, uploading an invoice). As they level up, they gain higher credibility, turning "compliance" into a rewarding experience.

### E. Statutory Deadline Alerts
*   **What it is:** Proactive banner notifications for critical filings (e.g., SARS/IRS Form 990).
*   **How it helps:** It ensures the NGO remains legally compliant, protecting their tax-exempt status and donor trust.

## 5. Technical Stack
*   **Frontend:** Kotlin & Jetpack Compose (Material 3) for a reactive, modern interface.
*   **Backend:** Firebase Firestore for real-time, cross-platform data synchronization.
*   **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) to ensure the app is scalable and maintainable.

## 6. Implementation Roadmap
*   **Phase 1 (Current):** MVP with Live Ledger, Donor Portal, and simulated XP systems.
*   **Phase 2:** Integration of a true Blockchain ledger for immutable, third-party verifiable records.
*   **Phase 3:** Automated beneficiary verification using IoT sensors and SMS gateway confirmations.
